3-SAT:
input: An expression of m clauses

->

pi
input: n+1 cards
a u and -u column on each

Make a hole punched out the u col if
ith clause of 3-SAT can be true
and the -u covered.
Cover the u col hole if the ith clause
can not be true (and -u punched)

Example:

Literal set: {u1,u2,u3,-u1,-u2,-u3}

(u1 or u2 or -u3) and (-u1 or u2 or u3) and (u1 or u2 or u3)

For each value u, line up each column corresponding to u and -u.
The amount of u's = the number of cards -1
So, |U| = n

Example above n = 3
because there is u1, u2 and u3
The expression above has 3 clauses
so, m = 3
