1b) I made the three real life objects in question entities. I decided
this because a book, patron, and staff are all tangible objects in the real
world. I added the Library as an entity because it is what will keep track
of the employees, patrons, and books.
The relationships between these entities are based on my experience going to
a library. At the simplest state a library member goes to the library, checks
out one or more books with their library card and staff, then later brings back
the books to return.
The attributes of each entity are simple and can be represented as a primitive.

1c) To ensure sufficient supply. A patron can only borrow up to 5 books at a time.
A patron must return or renew a book before the due date.
Only patrons can borrow books

1d) Borrow Books Use Case Scenario:

1. A patron searches the library book catalogue
2. The patron chooses books to be borrowed and brings it to a librarian staff
3. The librarian scans the book ISBN and enters the patrons library id card
4. The system ensures no more than 5 books are checked out by this library id card
5. The staff submits the request to borrow
6. The system marks the books being borrowed as being borrowed
7. The due date to return the books is generated by the system
